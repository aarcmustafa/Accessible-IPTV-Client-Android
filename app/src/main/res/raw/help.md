# Start

Open Playlists. Add an Xtream login, an M3U URL, an M3U file, or a Stalker portal (address and MAC). Xtream and Stalker bring their own guide. An M3U that names its guide brings it too; otherwise add its guide URL. A whole Xtream panel link pasted as the server fills in the login.

# Main screen

Search is at the top. It searches every group in the section; exact names come first. Voice, next to it, fills it by speech. Back clears it.

Below: Live TV, Movies, Series. Then Playlists, On now, Guide search, Guide, Recordings, More.

Groups are on the left, the list on the right. Both have headings. Picking a group moves you to the list.

Number keys on a remote or keyboard jump to that channel number.

# More

Previous channel, Recently watched, Go to channel number, Refresh playlists, Refresh guide, Account, Settings, Check for updates, Help.

# Items

Activate to play. A series opens its episodes.

Actions menu: play in another app, favourite, details, guide, record or stop recording, download. Without TalkBack: long-press, or the Menu key.

# Guide

On now lists everything airing. A channel's guide plays past programmes marked catch-up, and schedules future ones. Guide search finds upcoming programmes.

# Player

Buttons: Pause, Audio, Subtitles, Record, Cast, Full screen, Close. Keys: play/pause, A for audio, S for subtitles, F for full screen.

Full screen hides the buttons. Tap, Back, OK or F brings them back.

Audio description is picked when a channel has it. A track you pick is remembered per channel.

Subtitles show under the video and are spoken, unless Speak subtitles is off in Settings.

When the programme changes, the player says what is on now. Settings, Announcements sets how much the player says.

A live stream that drops reconnects by itself.

# Recordings

Saved to Movies/AccessibleIPTV. Recordings in progress are listed first; activate one to stop it.

Scheduled recordings run 2 minutes past the end and survive a restart. Change the minutes before and after in Settings.

A catch-up download that ends early is fetched again.

# Settings

Export settings saves playlists, logins, favourites and options to a file. Import settings loads one, on this device or another.

# Updates

The app checks for a new version once a day. More, Check for updates checks now. Android asks you to confirm every install.
